# CSG audit (read-only, not production visuals)

All 7,134 UnionOperation and 1,055 IntersectOperation instances have external AssetId references (1,235 unique). Embedded MeshData/MeshData2 and ChildData/ChildData2 are empty throughout. Shared physics data is NOT render mesh data. Read-only local decoder parsed all 1,074 distinct non-box version 6/7 CSGPHS payloads: 6,729 instance references, 32,426 convex hulls, 994,544 triangles. 790 box collision markers, 670 empty physics fields; no fabricated geometry for either. Five aerodynamic shared payloads (54 instance references) also decoded into vertex/index arrays; they do not establish visual fidelity. Diagnostic GLBs kept in separate local archive, not production asset library. No new requests against previously unresolved MeshIds; 1,952 MeshId strings originally inventoried, 38 priority IDs probed previously, 3 resolved and 35 returned 401. Untested refs are not assumed inaccessible. CSG external AssetIds are separate from MeshId references. Lockers, bathroom stalls/fixtures, barriers, most fences, full palms/beechwood, vehicles and sofa remain incomplete or unresolved. No procedural substitute presented as harvested.

Format references: https://devforum.roblox.com/t/some-info-on-sharedstrings-for-custom-collision-data-meshparts-unions-etc/294588 and https://github.com/Windows81/Roblox-Freedom-Distribution-Guides/blob/main/CSGv3Research/README.md. Original render geometry fields were tested directly in the source RBXL, not inferred from the spec.
